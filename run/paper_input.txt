Title: Singlet color center in oxygen-deficient TiO2

p1:2 We report an experimental and computational study of a color center in oxygen-deficient TiO2. The defect gives rise to a singlet ground state and is associated with oxygen vacancies in both rutile and anatase phases.
p1:3 Electron paramagnetic resonance and optical spectroscopy were used to characterize the defect, and density functional theory (DFT) calculations were performed to identify the atomic structure.
p2:1 The defect exhibits a characteristic absorption band in the visible range, which is consistent with the presence of oxygen vacancies.
p2:2 DFT calculations indicate that the singlet state originates from two electrons trapped at adjacent oxygen vacancy sites, rather than from a single isolated vacancy.
p2:3 These findings suggest that oxygen vacancies can create defect states inside the band gap, which may enhance visible-light absorption in TiO2.
p3:1 The exact contribution of this color center to photocatalytic activity under real conditions remains to be quantified (Ref. [7]), and generalizability to other morphologies was not examined (Ref. [12]).
